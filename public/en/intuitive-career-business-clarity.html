A few useful answers
Do I need to know exactly what I want to do?

No. You can arrive with a question, several ideas or a sense that your current direction no longer fits. The questionnaire helps us choose a useful focus before the session.

What does the one-page map include?

Your main strengths, the links we identify between your skills, a direction worth exploring and one practical next step. It is a concise synthesis you can return to after the conversation.
