Behind the music

Music that starts in the body.

My music lives somewhere between soul, jazz, rhythm & blues and the rhythms that have always made the body move before the mind has time to understand.

I write about love, freedom, self-return, feminine sovereignty and the quiet transformations that change the direction of a life.

Even when a song begins with heartbreak, desire or uncertainty, I am never interested in writing against someone.

I am interested in what we become after we have lived through it.

My story

There has always been more than one way for me to create.

Music is part of a wider world I have been building around identity, intuition, transformation and the ways we learn to return to ourselves.

A song may begin with a melody, a rhythm, a beat I record before it disappears, a sentence or simply a feeling. I follow it from there.

What matters to me is not how quickly I can create another song. What matters is whether the song deserves to stay with someone.
