Morgane Girault

There has always been more than one way for me to create.

I am an artist, medium and soul cartographer. I connect what we sense, the stories we carry and the forms we can give them in the world.

The invisible gives meaning. Embodiment gives transformation.

My mediumship and energetic reading are central to my work. I listen for the patterns, connections and movements that are not always easy to bring together in a conscious story.

Then comes the question I care about: what can this become in your decisions, your work, your relationships and the way you express yourself?

Music is another language for the same movement.

I write about love, freedom, desire and returning to yourself. A song can begin with a rhythm, a phrase, a melody or a feeling. I follow the thread until it finds a form.

And ideas need a practical home.

My technical and creative skills also help people express an offer, improve a digital journey and build a useful way of working. I work online internationally from Da Nang, Vietnam, in English and French.
