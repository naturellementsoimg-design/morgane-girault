My Story

My name is Morgane Girault. I’m 33 years old, a single mother of two, and the founder of a leadership and identity recalibration practice for ambitious women.

But none of this started in the world of mentoring.

I’ve built multiple businesses from the ground up. A cosmetic boutique in Paris. A beauty institute in France. I learned structure, operations, branding, sales - and what it truly takes to build something tangible.

Along the way, something unexpected kept happening.

Women were coming to me - not just for services, but for clarity. For direction. For perspective. They asked for advice on their life decisions, their positioning, their identity shifts, their business moves.

I realized I wasn’t just building companies.

I was recalibrating people.

And eventually, I made a radical decision.

I walked away from what was stable. I left the predictable path. I moved across continents with my children. And I fully committed to what had always been there beneath the surface:

My ability to see beyond the surface.

My work today is a fusion of grounded strategy and intuitive depth.

I am deeply intuitive. My mediumship is not performative - it’s perceptive. I detect patterns beneath words. I see generational dynamics. I identify energetic blocks and unspoken fears. I understand how identity trauma can shape decision-making.

But I don’t stop at awareness.

I build structured recalibration protocols.

I create custom workbooks during mentorship containers. I guide women through decisive action plans. I help them move from internal clarity to external execution.

Who I Work With

I work with ambitious women who have already achieved something - but feel the ceiling.

They don’t need motivation.

They need recalibration.
