Privacy Policy

Welcome to the website of Morgane Girault. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal data is collected, used, and protected in connection with our mentoring and professional advisory services.

1. Personal Data We Collect

We collect only the data necessary for the proper delivery of our services, including:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Billing and payment information
  • Communication data related to the mentoring relationship

This information is collected through direct exchanges (email, calls, forms) and through secure payment platforms.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

Your data is used to:

  • Manage registrations and client relationships
  • Handle invoicing and payment tracking
  • Communicate about services and respond to inquiries
  • Improve services and provide relevant offers

3. Legal Basis

The processing of your data is based on:

  • The performance of a contract (mentorship and advisory services)
  • Your consent when contacting or purchasing services
  • Compliance with legal and accounting obligations

4. Data Retention

Your data is retained for the duration necessary to manage our relationship and archived according to legal requirements (up to 10 years for accounting purposes where applicable).

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:

  • Use of secure payment platforms (Stripe)
  • Password-protected systems
  • Restricted access to authorized persons only

6. Data Sharing

Your data is never sold. It may only be shared with trusted service providers involved in delivering our services (e.g., Stripe for payments).

7. Cookies

This website uses cookies strictly necessary for its operation and may use third-party analytics tools for anonymous traffic analysis. You may manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

8. Your Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable laws, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations)
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Request data portability

To exercise these rights, contact: Morgane.girault@naturellement-soi.com

9. Limitation of Liability

The services provided by Morgane Girault are intended to support reflection, decision-making, and professional positioning. They do not replace medical, psychological, financial, or legal advice. Clients remain fully responsible for their decisions and actions.

10. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. The online version is always the current version. We encourage you to review it regularly.
